Calendar Mirror

Mirrors events from one or more calendars one-way into a target calendar, e.g. from Exchange (Owl) to Nextcloud, to see them on your phone. Changes in the sources are followed; nothing is ever written back to the sources.

Sources

Calendars whose events are mirrored. The order sets the priority: if the same event exists in several sources, the version from the higher source is mirrored.

    Target

    Ideally a separate, empty calendar used only for the mirror. The mirror creates copies with a UID calmirror-… there and only changes or deletes such copies; other events in the target are left alone. When you switch to another target, the copies in the previous target stay there and have to be deleted manually.

    Reminders are enabled for the target calendar while reminders are being mirrored. Thunderbird will then show every reminder twice (original and copy). To fix this, turn off “Show Reminders” in the target calendar's properties. Your phone will still show the reminders.

    Time range

    Events with at least one occurrence in this range are mirrored. Recurring events are mirrored as a whole. Copies that fall out of the range are deleted from the target.

    Adjustments to the copies

    These adjustments are based on observations with Exchange calendars via the Owl add-on. They may be unnecessary or unsuitable for other sources. Changing them rewrites all copies on the next sync.

    What is always adjusted
    • The copy gets its own UID (calmirror-…) plus the fields X-MIRROR-SOURCE-UID, X-MIRROR-SOURCE-CALENDAR and X-MIRROR-HASH, which the mirror uses to match source and copy.
    • DTSTAMP, LAST-MODIFIED and CREATED are dropped. Owl rewrites them on every sync; otherwise every copy would be rewritten all the time.
    • An invalid “Show time as” value (TRANSP, delivered by Owl as unset) becomes “Busy”.

    Sync

    Changes in the sources are followed immediately; the periodic full sync also moves the time range along. After Thunderbird starts, some calendars (e.g. Owl) need a few minutes until all events are loaded. During that time nothing is deleted in the target, only created and updated.
